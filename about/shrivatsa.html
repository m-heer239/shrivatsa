About Us

Who We Are

At Super Sonic Import Export, we don’t just trade products - we build trust, industries, and long-term value. Our journey began as Sonic Traders and evolved into Shree Sonic Polymers, a trusted name in India’s industrial sector for nearly two decades, delivering premium plastic granules and industrial solutions through strong partnerships and consistent excellence.

Today, we stand as Super Sonic Import Export - a global enterprise connecting industries across Europe, Asia, and beyond. At our core is Shrivatsa, our premium brand built on trust, quality, innovation, and sustainability. Shrivatsa represents our legacy elevated - redefining global trade with precision, reliability, and ambition. From local roots to global impact, Shrivatsa is our mark of excellence in a future without boundaries.

Our Sustainability Philosophy

At Shrivatsa, sustainability is not a marketing statement - it is a responsibility embedded in how we operate.

We believe that long-term business success depends on:

  • Responsible use of resources
  • Ethical sourcing and manufacturing
  • Environmentally conscious supply chain decisions

Our approach focuses on practical action, transparency, and continuous improvement, aligned with evolving European and global sustainability standards.

Our Sustainable Actions

We actively implement sustainability across multiple areas of our operations:

  • Eco-friendly paper and biodegradable product ranges, including responsibly sourced bagasse-based solutions
  • Plastic and metal scrap recycling programs to support circular material usage
  • Optimized logistics planning designed to reduce unnecessary transportation and associated carbon emissions
  • Partnerships with ethically aligned suppliers who meet quality, safety, and environmental expectations
  • Continuous research and evaluation to improve material efficiency, durability, and waste reduction

These actions are regularly reviewed to ensure consistency, compliance, and long-term environmental responsibility.

Responsible Supply Chain Commitment

Sustainability is closely linked to supply chain reliability.

We work with vetted manufacturing partners and suppliers who demonstrate:

  • Compliance with applicable regulations
  • Consistent quality standards
  • Responsible production practices

Our focus is on building stable, long-term supply relationships that support both business continuity and environmental accountability.

Our Ongoing Commitment

Environmental responsibility is an evolving process.

Shrivatsa remains committed to:

  • Monitoring regulatory developments across European markets
  • Improving material selection and product lifecycle performance
  • Enhancing operational efficiency to reduce waste and environmental impact

We believe sustainable progress is achieved through discipline, consistency, and long-term thinking.

Specific certifications, test reports, and compliance documentation are available upon request, depending on product category and market requirements.
